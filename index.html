Start Crate Digging

Some of the best records are the ones you stumble across at a flea market. When an album catches your eye, take a minute to look at the vinyl and its sleeves before you buy it.

What to Inspect

Start with the record itself, then check the outer jacket and the inner sleeve that holds the vinyl.

Record Surface

Hold the record by its edges and tilt it under a light. Check both sides for deep scratches and look along the edge for warping.

Outer Jacket

Look for split seams, worn corners, and signs of water damage.

Inner Sleeve

Check whether the inner sleeve is torn, dirty, or missing. A worn inner sleeve is easy to replace.

Before You Buy

Run through these three checks before you take the record to the counter.

  1. Check that the record label matches the album cover.
  2. Ask whether you can hear a sample if you’re unsure about its condition.
  3. Decide what you’re comfortable paying for the copy in your hands.
